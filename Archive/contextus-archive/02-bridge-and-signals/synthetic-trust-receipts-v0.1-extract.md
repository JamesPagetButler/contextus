## Purpose

This document is a **validation exercise**, not a design commitment. All parameter values are provisional and subject to empirical tuning across multiple domains.

The goal: take known historical insights from QBP, Materia-Bio, and ecology programmes, reconstruct what their Trust Receipts *would have* contained at the moment of initial observation, trace the heartbeat trajectory against actual epistemic history, and use discrepancies to calibrate bridge parameters.

## Method

For each insight:
1. Reconstruct what the Trust Receipt *would have* contained at the moment of initial observation.
2. Trace the heartbeat trajectory: did the underlying signal strengthen, weaken, or stay silent?
3. Trace the CTH-internal evidence: what derivations, measurements, or confluences arrived independently?
4. Compare predicted ρ trajectory against known epistemic status in CTH inventory v3.2.
5. Note where the dynamics produce the right answer and where they don't.

## Toward Default Logic

The non-QBP receipts revealed that three parameters previously flagged as "domain-configurable" may actually be functions of **measurable properties of the insight itself**, not of domain.

### Observation 1: Decay half-life tracks evidence cycle time, not domain

| Receipt | Domain | Expected evidence cadence | Appropriate half-life |
|---|---|---|---|
| SR-01 | QBP | Weeks (computational verification) | Short — fast confirmation or rejection |
| SR-02 | QBP | Months-to-years (rare publications) | Long |
| SR-03 | QBP | Unknown (waiting for GRB follow-up) | Long, but for different reasons than SR-02 |
| SR-06 | Ecology | Seasonal (~6 months) | Matched to season length |
| SR-07 | Genomics | Months-to-years (genome sequencing) | Long |
| SR-08 | Marine | Stochastic (external fieldwork) | Very long or cadence-unaware |

The pattern is not "physics = 90 days, ecology = 180 days." It's "how soon could relevant evidence plausibly arrive?" Two QBP receipts (SR-01 and SR-02) need very different half-lives despite being in the same domain.

**Candidate default logic:**

```
evidence_cycle_time = estimate from:
  1. Historical activation frequency of source nodes in Contextus
  2. Publication cadence of provenance sources
  3. (External) field season schedule for ecological/observational claims
  4. Theoretical-only flag (no observational cadence)

decay_half_life = f(evidence_cycle_time)
```

### Observation 2: Heartbeat bit fraction tracks evidence independence

A new paper carrying confirming theoretical derivation is not the same epistemic event as a second eDNA sample showing the same correlation. Both are "strengthening signals" in the bridge sense, but they carry different weight:

- **Independent confirmation** (different team, different method, different domain) → high bit fraction
- **Replication** (same method, second sample) → low bit fraction
- **Parameter refinement** (more precise measurement of an established quantity) → very low bit fraction

The bridge needs to record source independence as a property of each heartbeat event.

### Observation 3: Internal evidence resistance tracks evidence formality

Derivation chains in formal mathematics are stronger than correlative chains in ecology. A formal proof reduces decay risk dramatically; a statistical correlation reduces it modestly. The resistance factor should be tier-aware and formality-aware.
