## Key decisions captured here

1. **eDNA is a layer in Contextus, not a sub-programme.** From Contextus's view, it's another sensing modality alongside satellite, weather, camera traps.

2. **eDNA is a theoretical lens for Materia-Bio.** The community-as-morphotype analogy is the centrepiece — the claim that community assembly rules may be structurally analogous to developmental regulatory networks.

3. **Cross-programme handshake protocol.** Contextus passes observational anomalies to Materia-Bio as mechanism questions; Materia-Bio returns testable predictions. Neither programme owns the loop. The loop is the bridge.

4. **The geothermal gradient experiment** was identified as the lowest-cost / highest-information flagship: existing access, characterised gradients, standard methods, directly tests bistable-switch vs. neutral-assembly hypotheses for community states.
